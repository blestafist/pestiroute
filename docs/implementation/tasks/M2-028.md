# M2-028 — Add bounded-backpressure conformance cases

## Outcome

A slow consumer bounds producer progress rather than accumulating a complete response. Both native-loopback and scripted Connector implementations pass identical contract scenarios under slow or paused consumption. See [registry](../TASKS.md#m2--connector-api-and-conformance-baseline) and [M2](../ROADMAP.md#m2--connector-api-and-conformance-baseline).

## Scope

- Expand `internal/conformance/` (e.g. `backpressure_test.go` or `conformance_test.go`) with shared bounded-backpressure scenarios executed against both `native-loopback` and `scripted` connector factories:
  - Stalled consumer bounds producer progress: consumer halts or delays frame reads (`Stream.Next()`) after initial delivery; verify producer step/read progression is bounded without accumulating subsequent frames in memory or unbounded queues.
  - Resumed consumer delivers verbatim bytes: consumer resumes reading to terminal completion; verify exact byte-level payload preservation in original sequence order without corruption, drop, or frame reordering.
  - Interruption under stall: context cancellation while the downstream reader is stalled unblocks waiting producer goroutines promptly, releases connector/stream resources, and finalizes the attempt exactly once as cancelled without leaking goroutines.
  - Rejection of anti-conformance buffering: verify conformance assertions reject non-conforming connectors that buffer future frames during downstream stalls.
- Explicit limits:
  - Do not invent artificial performance budgets or flaky timing-only sleeps; use deterministic synchronization (`Gate`, `Waiting`, `Sent`, step counters).
  - Test-only additions under `internal/conformance/`; no changes to `internal/core/` or `internal/connector/` unless fixing conformance assertion coverage.
  - Synthetic and local loopback fixtures only; no live provider credentials or external networks.

## Acceptance

- [x] Conformance scenarios execute against both `native-loopback` and `scripted` connector factories under paused/stalled consumer conditions.
- [x] While downstream consumption is paused, producer progress/read count is bounded (no whole-response accumulation or unbounded queuing of unconsumed frames).
- [x] Resuming consumption receives the complete response verbatim and in exact order without frame loss, corruption, or duplication.
- [x] Context cancellation during a consumer stall unblocks waiting execution promptly, releases resources, and finalizes the attempt exactly once as cancelled without leaking goroutines.
- [x] Conformance runner synchronization uses channel-based gating (`Gate`/`Waiting`/`Sent`) and step counters rather than arbitrary sleeps or timing thresholds.
- [x] Deliberate anti-conformance violations (e.g. whole-response buffering during stall) are rejected by assertion-bearing conformance checks.

For native-loopback, the producer sends 32 MiB of ungated SSE body steps with a per-step `Sent` observation. The assertion allows unspecified OS socket readahead and rejects completion before initial delivery; it does not claim a universal chunk/byte threshold. For scripted, exactly Head and the first Body may have been consumed at the pause point (bound 2); the fixture is pull-driven and has no internal queue. The same assertion rejects a deterministic 64-step read-ahead mutation. `fakeupstream.Step.Waiting` signals entry at a closed gate, directly tested to precede `Sent` until release.
