## Scope

- Exercise the assembled protected Codex route and SQLite ledger across durable settlement, interruption, auth failure, and restart reconciliation:
  - Streaming success with complete usage persistence: verify `settled` reservation state, truthful effective charge, and distinct counters (input, output, reasoning, cached tokens).
  - Stream interruption, truncation, or client cancellation: verify partial usage recording, conservative reservation settlement, and exactly-once accounting.
  - Envelope completion idempotency: verify repeated or duplicate Complete calls on execution envelope cannot duplicate charges, release reservations twice, or double-settle.
  - Auth persistence failure or storage failure during dispatch: verify that persistence failure suppresses internal completion, blocks inference, and fails closed with health/readiness withdrawal per storage-failure policy.
  - Abrupt restart recovery: verify that gateway restart with uncommitted dispatch intent or in-flight attempts reconciles ledger state cleanly without duplicate charges or fabricated free work.
- Explicit limits: No accounting schema redesign, no live network calls, no speculative client retry, and no cross-account state migration.
