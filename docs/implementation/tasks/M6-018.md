# M6-018 — Bridge incremental Execute output

## Outcome

Bridge incremental `core.Connector.Execute` output over gRPC IPC, delivering ordered partial `StreamFrame` chunks (`Head`, incremental `BodyChunk`, and `Complete`) to the consumer before producer completion without response buffering or payload aggregation. See [registry](../TASKS.md#m6--external-plugin-runtime) and [milestone](../ROADMAP.md#m6--external-plugin-runtime).

## Scope

- Host-side stream reception in `internal/connector/external/execute_linux.go`:
  - Ensure `executeResponseStream.Next(ctx)` yields incremental `core.FrameBody` frames as they arrive from `stream.Recv()` before the child finishes or closes its stream.
  - Enforce stream lifecycle invariants: `Head` followed by zero or more `Body` frames, followed by exactly one `Complete` frame, followed by `io.EOF`.
  - Enforce terminal frame semantics: reject unexpected frames arriving after `CompleteFrame` or before `HeadFrame`.
  - Preserve exact opaque body bytes across chunk boundaries without aggregation or buffering.
- Child test harness in `internal/connector/external/handshake_linux_test.go`:
  - Add gated streaming fixture: child sends `HeadFrame` and first partial `BodyChunk`, waits on a synchronization gate confirming the caller observed the early frame before child sends remaining body chunks and `CompleteFrame`.
  - Add split-byte fixture: multi-chunk payload where arbitrary byte streams (multi-byte runes, NUL/0xff bytes, chunks across 64 KiB boundaries) are split across consecutive frames and delivered incrementally.
  - Add terminal frame fixtures: child emitting valid `CompleteFrame` with non-succeeded outcomes (`OutcomeFailed`, `OutcomeCancelled`, `OutcomeIncomplete`) with classified error/usage metadata, and child emitting invalid post-Complete frames.
- Tests in `internal/connector/external/execute_linux_test.go`:
  - Verify early delivery: consumer observes `Head` and first `Body` while producer is paused/holding the remainder.
  - Verify split-byte preservation across multiple chunks without buffering.
  - Verify terminal frames: outcomes, usage reports, and rejection of frames after Complete.
- Limits: Single-invocation cancellation and deadlines are M6-019. Slow-reader backpressure and concurrency saturation bounds are M6-020. Child crash isolation/recovery is M6-021/M6-022. Native Responses child composition root is M6-024.

## Acceptance
